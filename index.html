Ang Kwento ng PiliKart

"Pumili Ka + Cart."

Where every click is a step closer to home.

We didn't just build an e-commerce platform; we built a digital love letter to the Filipino heritage. In a world that is constantly rushing forward, PiliKart is our way of looking back—honoring the kalan and banga of our ancestors, while embracing the limitless possibilities of our future.

Here, you don't just shop. You experience the warmth of a Filipino tahanan.

Apat na Tindahan, Isang Pusong Pinoy

To truly understand who we are, you must meet our four pillars. Each one is named after a piece of our history, a testament to the ingenuity and resilience of the Filipino people.

PASO (Food Essentials)

Named after the banga (clay pot) that represents our beloved Calamba, Laguna. PASO is the heart of the kitchen—bringing you the comfort of lutong-bahay and the flavors that remind you of home.

NIPA (Home Appliances)

Derived from the Bahay Kubo, NIPA is built on the belief that a house is not a home without the warmth of family. We offer the essentials that turn your space into a sanctuary, no matter how simple.

HABI (Fashion)

In Tagalog, habi means to weave. Just as our ancestors wove the fabric of our culture thread by thread, HABI brings you clothing and fashion that reflect our vibrant identity—where tradition meets modern confidence.

APOLLO (Technologies)

Did you know that the Lunar Roving Vehicle used by Apollo missions was allegedly designed by Eduardo San Juan, a Filipino NASA engineer? APOLLO celebrates this boundless Filipino ingenuity, bringing you tech essentials that prove we can reach for the stars while remaining rooted in our soil.

Ang Malasakit sa Bawat Transaksyon

We know that genuine care means looking out for everyone. In PiliKart, inclusivity isn't an afterthought—it is the foundation. That is why we built in layered discounts, offering special rates for our Senior Citizens and Persons with Disabilities (PWDs).

We also understand the modern Filipino's journey. Whether you are an OFW sending love back home, a foreign friend exploring our culture, or a local supporting local, PiliKart speaks your language. With our integrated multi-currency system (PHP, USD, EUR, JPY), shopping for the things you love has never been more accessible.

Ang PILI Kahera: Ang Iyong Digital Na Bulsa

Shopping shouldn't be complicated. That is why we created PILI Kahera, your virtual ATM and wallet all in one.

We wanted to make financial management as simple and pleasant as possible. Need to check your balance? Top up your wallet for a big purchase? Or maybe you need to withdraw funds? PILI Kahera handles it all securely, ensuring your digital wallet is always ready to serve you. It’s not just about money; it’s about giving you peace of mind so you can focus on what matters—finding joy in your purchases.

Bakit PiliKart?

Because the future is local.

Every time you choose PiliKart, you aren't just adding to a cart. You are supporting local businesses. You are keeping the piso circulating within our communities. You are empowering Filipino farmers, artisans, and entrepreneurs.

Will your journey begin at the comforting embrace of HABI, or will you take flight with APOLLO?

Mga Tanong

Frequently Asked Questions

Paano ako mag-order?

Pumili ka ng produkto mula sa menu, i-click ang + Add, tapos pumunta sa cart para mag-checkout. Pwede kang mag-order bilang guest o mag-sign in muna.

Anong payment methods ang tinatanggap niyo?

Cash on Delivery (COD) at GCash. Kung GCash, kailangan mong i-upload ang resibo sa checkout para ma-verify ang bayad.

Magkano ang delivery fee?

Ang standard delivery fee ay ₱50 sa loob ng Calamba, Laguna. Ito ay makikita mo sa order summary bago ka mag-confirm.

Gaano katagal bago dumating ang order?

Karaniwang 1–2 araw sa loob ng Calamba. Pwede mong i-track ang status sa My Orders page — Pending, Shipping, o Delivered.

Paano ko malalaman kung out-of-stock ang isang item?

Sa product card, makikita mo ang bilang ng stock. Kapag zero na, awtomatikong naka-disable ang + Add button at "Out" ang label nito.

Pwede bang mag-cancel ng order?

Bago pa ma-ship ang order mo, pwede kang mag-message sa amin sa pamamagitan ng PiliBot para i-cancel. Kapag na-ship na, hindi na pwede.

Ano ang Sunset Mode?

Ito ang dark theme ng PiliKart. Buksan ang navigation menu at i-tap ang "Sunset" para makita ang system sa mas malamig na kulay. Naka-save ito sa browser mo.

Sino si PiliBot?

Si PiliBot ang aming friendly chat assistant sa kanang-baba ng screen. Tanungin mo siya tungkol sa delivery, GCash, o Budget Mode.
